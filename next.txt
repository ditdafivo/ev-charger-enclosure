Task 3 [completed]: Power junction box and conduit runs modeling

A 1-1/4" conduit riser emerges from the ground at
x=15 and y=6, immediately in front of front_center_rail. It enters
the bottom center of a Carlon E987N 4x4x4 PVC junction box whose
bottom face is 6 inches (parameterized) above the local ground plane.
The box is mounted with its rear face against front_center_rail.

The junction box has four solvent-welded penetration assemblies:

* Bottom center: E996G 1-1/4" box adapter and E940G coupling for the riser.
* Rear: E996G 1-1/4" box adapter, E940G coupling, and a short horizontal
  1-1/4" conduit run entering an E986G Type LB body, with an E950GF
  1-1/4"-to-1" reducer in the upward outlet.
* Top: E996D 1/2" box adapter and E940D coupling for the street light run.
* Right: E996D 1/2" box adapter and E940D coupling for the outlet run.

The rear EV penetration is at x=15.875, y=7, z=8.4. A straight 1-1/4"
horizontal run extends in the positive-y direction from the E940G coupling to
the E986G LB inlet at approximately y=10.392. The LB's upward hub and E950GF
reducer are centered at y=12.15, directly below the EV charger entry. The 1"
vertical rise remains at y=12.15 and uses a small x offset from x=15.875 to
x=16 before entering the charger body at z=21.65.

The E986G is marked 32 cubic inches. The three insulated #6 conductors and
single #6 equipment-grounding conductor passing through it require 20 cubic
inches under the same conservative fill method used for the junction box,
leaving 12 cubic inches of margin. The installed conduit-body marking must be
verified before construction.

The top 1/2" street light penetration is at x=13.75, y=4 so the fitting and
conduit remain forward of the open tambour door at y=5.5. The run follows a
smooth forward and lateral offset before rising into the open bottom port of
the WRB550B street light base box.

The 1/2" outlet run leaves the right side of the box and runs in the
positive x direction. At the back_right_outlet hub center x=27.016
it uses a standard 4"-radius 90-degree sweep toward the rear, followed by
a second standard sweep upward directly beneath the outlet entry. Minor
framing holes or notches needed by the physical installation are not modeled.

The input contains four 6 AWG THHN and four 12 AWG THHN conductors. Three
non-ground 6 AWG conductors continue unspliced to the charger. The incoming
12 AWG hot/hot/neutral conductors feed separate light and outlet hot/neutral
pairs, and the equipment grounds are spliced. The conservative box-fill
calculation is 37.00 cubic inches against an assumed 49.00 cubic inch marked
E987N capacity. The installed box marking and locally applicable electrical
code must be verified before construction. Required fitting materials are:

* 1 x Carlon E987N 4x4x4 PVC junction box
* 1 x E996G 1-1/4" box adapter and 1 x E940G 1-1/4" coupling
* 1 x E996G 1-1/4" box adapter, 1 x E940G 1-1/4" coupling, a field-cut
  1-1/4" PVC conduit stub, 1 x E986G Type LB conduit body, and 1 x E950GF
  1-1/4"-to-1" reducer bushing
* 2 x E996D 1/2" box adapters and 2 x E940D 1/2" couplings
* Carlon-compatible PVC primer and solvent cement as required

Task 6 [completed]: Low voltage cable modeling

Three 1/8"-diameter low voltage cables exit the bottom cable glands. All
turns have a centerline radius of at least five cable diameters (0.625"),
using broader turns where the surrounding geometry allows.

Path 1 leaves its gland downward, sweeps toward the positive-x face of post_fl,
follows the underside of brace_fl_fr clear of the street-light backing, and
ends in a mirrored 1.25"-radius service loop centered directly above the
WRB550B/WRE450G street light box stack.

Paths 2 and 3 are blue Cat 6 cables. They leave their glands downward, pass
around opposite x sides of the 3/4-inch low-voltage ground riser, and sweep in
positive y to adjacent lanes on the negative-x face of front_center_rail. A
0.625-inch turn keeps their upward legs on the negative-y side of rail_fb until
they clear its top. The dark-brown street-light cable leaves its gland on a
gentle spline with a single low point at z=8.25 rather than a flat horizontal
run between two bends.

Path 2 continues to rail_ft, crosses its positive-y face in positive x,
descends the negative-x face of right_center_rail, and turns around the rail's
positive-y face just below the wifi access point. It then sweeps in positive x
and z into the existing bottom entry. Path 3 branches from the shared
front_center_rail climb near the bottom plane of ev_charger_body, clears the
rail in positive y, and sweeps upward into the width-and-depth center of the
charger's bottom entry.
